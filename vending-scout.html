    How it works

    Scores run from 0 to 100. They combine the type of place (hospitals, gyms, apartments and colleges rank highest), building size when it's listed, weekly opening hours, and how many other busy sites sit within 500 m. Points come off for convenience stores, cafes, supermarkets and vending machines within 150 m.

    The data comes from OpenStreetMap, which has no visitor counts or reviews, and many places are missing hours or contact details. Use the ranking as a shortlist and check each site in person.

    Favorites and notes are saved on this device only. Address suggestions come from Photon, a search service built on OpenStreetMap. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
